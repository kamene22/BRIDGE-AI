# Statutory Minimum Wages in Kenya: Official Gazette Guide

*Authoritative legal guide on Kenya's statutory minimum wage framework, regional schedules, occupational categories, and employer compliance rules under the Regulation of Wages (General) Order.*

---

## 1. Statutory Framework and Legal Authority

Statutory minimum wages in Kenya are governed by the **Labour Institutions Act (No. 12 of 2007)** and the **Employment Act (No. 11 of 2007)** under subsidiary legislation promulgated as the **Regulation of Wages (General) Order**. 

The Cabinet Secretary responsible for Labour, acting on the recommendations of the **General Wages Advisory Board (GWAB)** and the **Agricultural Wages Advisory Board (AWAB)**, sets and periodically adjusts statutory basic minimum wages across Kenya.

- **Enforcement Authority:** Ministry of Labour and Social Protection (Department of Labour Inspectorate).
- **Statutory Binding Effect:** It is an offense under Kenyan law for an employer to pay any employee a basic wage below the gazetted minimum wage rate corresponding to their occupational category and geographical location.

---

## 2. Regional Minimum Wage Categories

Kenyan minimum wage legislation divides the country into three primary geographical zones to reflect differences in cost of living:

1. **Cities (Zone 1):** Nairobi City County, Mombasa City, and Kisumu City.
2. **Former Municipalities & Major Towns (Zone 2):** Nakuru, Eldoret, Thika, Nyeri, Kakamega, Kisii, Malindi, Kitale, Machakos, Meru, Kericho, and all former Municipalities.
3. **All Other Areas (Zone 3):** All rural areas and locations not falling under Zone 1 or Zone 2.

## 4. Employer Compliance and Enforcement

- **Mandatory Payslip Disclosure:** Employers must issue an itemized written pay statement (payslip) showing the gross basic wage, housing allowance, statutory deductions, and net pay (Section 20 of the Employment Act).
- **Penalties for Non-Compliance:** Any employer who pays less than the gazetted minimum wage commits an offense and is liable on conviction to a fine or imprisonment, in addition to being ordered by the Labour Court to pay the employee all accumulated wage arrears.
- **Reporting Mechanism:** Employees paid below statutory minimum wage levels can lodge a formal wage claim with the nearest **County Labour Office (Ministry of Labour and Social Protection)** or file a claim at the **Employment and Labour Relations Court (ELRC)**.
